# Product Vision

Voi helps casual badminton hosts organize recurring group sessions without messy chat threads, spreadsheets, or manual payment tracking.

## Positioning

Voi is not a general event platform and not a full venue booking marketplace.

It is a focused scheduling tool for badminton groups that want a fast, clean way to:

- Create a playing session.
- Invite familiar players.
- Confirm attendance.
- Handle waitlists.
- Organize players across courts.
- Split session costs.
- Remind people before the session starts.

## Target Users

### Primary User: Group Host

The group host organizes weekly or ad hoc badminton sessions. They usually coordinate through chat apps and manually track who joined, who cancelled, who paid, and how many courts are needed.

Host jobs:

- Create a session quickly.
- Share an invite link.
- Know who is confirmed.
- Replace cancelled players.
- Keep the session balanced and full.
- Track payment status.

### Secondary User: Player

The player wants to know whether there is a session, join quickly, get reminders, and avoid scrolling through long chat threads.

Player jobs:

- Join or decline quickly.
- See time, place, level, fee, and available slots.
- Know whether they are confirmed or waitlisted.
- Get notified when their status changes.

## Product Promise

Create a badminton session in under 30 seconds and let the app handle the rest of the coordination.

## Differentiation

Voi should feel closer to Lu.ma in simplicity and visual polish, but its workflows should be specific to badminton:

- Court-based capacity.
- Doubles-friendly lineup.
- Skill level cues.
- Waitlist replacement.
- Cost split by confirmed attendance.
- Recurring group sessions.

## Non-Goals

- Do not start as a marketplace for finding public games.
- Do not start with direct venue booking.
- Do not start with support for every sport.
- Do not start with complex league, tournament, or team management.
- Do not require players to create a heavy profile before joining a session.
